Effective Date: 2026/6/27

Refund Policy

At Optivolve Digital ("Optivolve", "we", "our", or "us"), we are committed to delivering reliable, secure, and high-quality financial technology solutions and digital payment services. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for services provided by Optivolve.

By engaging our services, you acknowledge and agree to this Refund Policy.

 

1. General Policy

Due to the nature of our technology and financial services, fees charged for successfully delivered services are generally non-refundable unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by applicable law.

Refund requests are assessed on a case-by-case basis and are subject to verification.

 

2. Eligible Refund Circumstances

A refund may be considered where:

  • A duplicate payment has been processed due to a verified system error.
  • An incorrect amount has been charged because of an error attributable to Optivolve.
  • A service has not been delivered due to a technical failure solely caused by Optivolve.
  • A commercial agreement specifically provides for a refund.

Approval of refunds remains at the sole discretion of Optivolve following internal review.

 

3. Non-Refundable Services

Refunds will generally not be issued where:

  • Services have been successfully delivered.
  • API integrations, development, implementation, or consulting work has commenced or been completed.
  • Delays arise from banking institutions, payment networks, telecommunications providers, or other third parties.
  • Regulatory reviews, compliance checks, or KYC verification result in transaction delays or rejections.
  • Incorrect information has been provided by the customer.
  • The customer changes their requirements after service delivery has commenced.
  • Suspension or termination occurs due to a breach of our Terms and Conditions.

 

4. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include:

  • Customer or company name
  • Transaction reference number
  • Invoice number (where applicable)
  • Date of transaction
  • Description of the issue
  • Supporting documentation

Requests should be submitted within 7 business days of the relevant transaction or invoice date.

 

5. Review Process

Each refund request is reviewed by our Finance and Operations teams. Additional information may be requested to facilitate the review process.

Submission of a refund request does not guarantee approval.

 

6. Processing Time

Approved refunds are generally processed within 7–14 business days, depending on the original payment method and applicable banking procedures.

Optivolve is not responsible for delays caused by financial institutions or payment providers.
